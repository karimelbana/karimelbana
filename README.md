## 🧭 How I work

- **Data before models.** A strong baseline and a clean, well-understood dataset beat a complex model on messy labels.
- **Measure what matters.** Pick metrics that match the business cost of errors: PR-AUC and per-class recall, not just accuracy.
- **Keep it simple and reproducible.** Small functions, clear names, tests, pinned environments.
- **Explain it.** Years of teaching and facilitation taught me that a model nobody understands rarely gets used.
